AI Use & Disclosure

Your mind is meant for creating.

Last updated September 21, 2026

No generative AI

There is no generative AI in try this one. Nothing writes for you, finishes your sentences, summarizes your notes or suggests ideas. Every word on your wall is one you put there.

Your ideas aren’t training data

Nothing you write, draw or record is sent to an AI service, and none of it is used to train an AI model — ours or anyone else’s.

The one piece of machine learning

Voice memos are transcribed with Apple’s built-in speech recognition, which turns what you said into text. It doesn’t add, change or generate anything, and on most phones it runs on the device itself.

Why

The blank space is where your own ideas come from. An app that filled it for you would be taking away the part that’s yours. try this one is built to hold your thoughts, not to have them for you.
