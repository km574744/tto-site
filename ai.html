AI Use & Disclosure

It runs on your phone, and it never writes for you.

Last updated September 21, 2026

It runs on the phone

Any model the app uses runs on the device. What you write, draw or record isn’t sent to an outside AI service, which means there’s nothing in transit and nothing of yours sitting on anybody else’s server. It also works with no signal. The one thing that can leave is voice transcription, below.

It doesn’t write for you

It won’t finish your sentences, suggest what to put down, or hand back a summary of your notes in words you didn’t use. Every word on your wall is one you put there.

Not training data

Nothing you write, draw or record is used to train a model — ours or anyone else’s.

Voice memos

Transcribed with Apple’s speech recognition, which turns what you said into text without adding to it. It runs on the phone when the phone can do it; where it can’t — some older devices and some languages — the audio goes to Apple to be transcribed.

Why it’s drawn this way

The blank space is the part that does the work. Anything that filled it in for you would be doing the one bit worth doing yourself.
