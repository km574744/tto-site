Your notes are on your phone

Everything saves to the phone itself, with no account needed. Signing in with Apple or Google adds a backup so you can pick things up on another phone — and signing out never takes anything off the phone.

Deleting your account

Open Settings from any tab, and under Account choose Delete account. Your account and the backup are deleted for good. What’s on the phone stays where it is.

What it doesn’t do

No model here writes for you, and any model the app runs, it runs on the phone. More about that here.
