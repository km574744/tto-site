Privacy Policy

What you write stays yours. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold.

Last updated September 22, 2026

On your phone

Your notes, lists, photos, drawings and voice memos are saved on this phone. No account needed.

If you sign in

Signing in with Apple or Google backs up your walls, tasks, photos, drawings and voice recordings to our backup provider, Supabase. We also keep the email associated with your account. Sign out to stop future backups.

Deleting your account

Settings, under Account: Delete account. Your account, the backup and anything stored with it are deleted for good. What’s on this phone is yours and stays.

Microphone and photos

Used only when you record or pick a photo. Voice memos are transcribed by Apple’s speech recognition, on your phone when it’s supported.

What isn’t here

No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs watching you use it. We do not sell your content or use it to train a model. Your content goes to our backup provider only if you sign in.
